# Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention Policy

Our app strictly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We enforce this through:
- Strong moderation and content filtering practices.
- User reporting tools and prompt review of flagged content.
- A zero-tolerance policy toward exploitative behavior.
- Immediate removal and reporting of any inappropriate content.

If you suspect any violation or need support, contact: crewcoad@gmail.com
